Split shared expenses without the math — or the fees.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind. ClanTab doesn't track you across other apps or sell your data to anyone.

No payment processing

Settling up is trust-based — ClanTab never touches your money and never asks for card or bank details.

Exact to the paisa

Every amount is tracked in whole cents/paise. No floating-point rounding, no money that quietly appears or disappears from the ledger.

Features

What it does

Quick sign-in

Sign in with Apple or Google — no email or password to create, nothing to verify. Then share a link or a 6-character code; anyone you add can join by name alone, no account required on their end.

Exact debt simplification

Collapses everyone's tangled IOUs into the smallest possible set of payments, so a group settles up with one or two transfers instead of six.

Multi-currency, kept honest

Add expenses in any supported currency. ClanTab never converts between them, so a balance can honestly read "owed €40 and owes $15."

Your data, exportable

Export any group to CSV or JSON at any time. The CSV also imports back in, including history from a Splitwise export.

How it works

Four steps to settled up

Sign in

With Apple or Google. No email or phone number needed.

Create or join a group

Share a link or a 6-character code — no account required for the people you add.

Add expenses

Split equally, by exact amount, or by percentage. ClanTab checks the math.

Settle up

See the fewest payments needed, mark them paid once you've settled outside the app.
